Veritiv Competitor Playbook v1

1) Packaging Distribution
Primary competitors:
- Uline
- Bunzl
- Imperial Dade
- International Paper / WestRock / Pratt channel programs (account dependent)

What they usually sell on:
- Commodity box pricing
- Fast shipping promises
- Broad SKU depth

How to win:
- Lead with total cost, not unit cost (damage, cube efficiency, labor)
- Push custom packaging optimization reviews
- Emphasize service consistency and account coverage

2) Facility / Jan-San / Breakroom / Safety
Primary competitors:
- Imperial Dade
- Bunzl
- BradyPLUS
- W.B. Mason
- Grainger (overlap)

What they usually sell on:
- Bundle discounts
- Private label substitutions
- Next-day footprint

How to win:
- Build contract around usage controls and standardization
- Show fill-rate and delivery reliability metrics
- Position cross-category consolidation savings

3) Print / Paper / Graphics
Primary competitors:
- Lindenmeyr Munroe
- Regional paper merchants
- Direct mill relationships

What they usually sell on:
- Mill relationships
- Grade availability
- Freight terms

How to win:
- Stress availability + forecasting + continuity planning
- Offer SKU rationalization by application
- Protect customer from supply disruptions with backup options

4) E-commerce / Fulfillment Packaging
Primary competitors:
- Uline
- Regional price-first packaging distributors
- 3PL-integrated supply channels

What they usually sell on:
- Speed and convenience
- Transactional web ordering

How to win:
- Lead with packaging workflow improvement (pick/pack efficiency)
- Offer right-size packaging program and damage reduction data
- Tie pricing to service levels and account support

5) National / Enterprise Account Bids
Primary competitors:
- Bunzl
- Imperial Dade
- W.B. Mason / Staples Business (scope dependent)
- Large regional consolidators

What they usually sell on:
- Aggressive contract pricing
- Procurement-friendly bid responses

How to win:
- Show measurable operating impact (fill rate, stockouts, labor)
- Bring executive cadence + reporting discipline
- Use pilot-first structure to prove value before full rollout

Fast objection handling lines
- If they say "Uline is cheaper":
  "On unit price, sometimes yes. On total delivered cost and process waste, we often win. Let's compare both." 

- If they say "We already have a vendor":
  "Great, we can run a low-risk benchmark on one category to validate savings and service differences." 

- If they say "We just need fast delivery":
  "Speed is table stakes. We also improve consistency and reduce packaging/labor waste over time." 

30-second qualification script
- "How are you buying today (single vendor vs multiple)?"
- "Where do you feel pain: price volatility, stockouts, service quality, or labor inefficiency?"
- "If we could improve one KPI this quarter, which matters most?"

Simple KPI set to track wins
- Fill rate
- On-time delivery
- Stockout incidents
- Damage/return rate
- Total monthly spend by category
- Labor time impact (if available)
