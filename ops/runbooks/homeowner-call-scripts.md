# Homeowner Lead Call Scripts (Voice Pack v1)

## Voice Roles
- **Avery** — Intake (first contact)
- **Mason** — Follow-up reminder
- **Elena** — Final gentle close-out / escalation

---

## 1) FIRST CONTACT SCRIPT (Avery – Intake)

### Live Answer Script
"Hi, this is **Avery** with **Tomlinson & Co** — thanks for your request.
Is now a bad time for a quick homeowners quote intake? It should only take 2–3 minutes."

**If yes (good time):**
"Great. I’ll make this easy. I just need a few details so we can prepare your options."

**Core questions (in order):**
1. "Can I confirm your full name?"
2. "What’s the property address you want quoted?"
3. "Best email and mobile for quote updates?"
4. "When does your current policy renew?"
5. "Who is your current carrier, if you know it?"
6. "Any recent updates to the home we should consider (roof, systems, renovations)?"

**Close:**
"Perfect — thank you. We’ll review options and follow up with next steps shortly.
Would you prefer text or email for updates?"

### If They’re Busy
"No problem at all. What’s the best time today for a 2-minute callback?
I can also send a quick secure link by text so you can finish when convenient."

### First Contact Voicemail (30–40 sec)
"Hi, this is **Avery** with **Tomlinson & Co**.
I’m following up on your homeowners quote request and can help you get options quickly.
Call or text us back at [NUMBER], and we’ll keep it simple.
Again, this is Avery with Tomlinson & Co at [NUMBER]."

---

## 2) SECOND FOLLOW-UP SCRIPT (Mason – Reminder)

### Live Answer Script
"Hi, this is **Mason** with **Tomlinson & Co** checking in on your homeowners quote request.
You’re close — I just need a couple details to finish your options.
Would you like to complete it now in under 2 minutes?"

**If hesitant:**
"Totally understand. I can send one quick text link and you can complete it on your own time — whichever is easier."

**If yes:**
"Perfect. Let’s finalize this now so we can move your quote forward."

**Close:**
"Great, you’re all set on intake. We’ll send next steps shortly.
If anything changes, just reply to our text/email."

### Second Follow-Up Voicemail
"Hi, this is **Mason** with **Tomlinson & Co**.
Quick follow-up on your homeowners quote request — we just need a couple details to finish your options.
Call or text us at [NUMBER], and we’ll make it fast and easy.
Again, Mason at Tomlinson & Co, [NUMBER]."

---

## 3) FINAL GENTLE CLOSE-OUT SCRIPT (Elena – Soft Close)

### Live Answer Script
"Hi, this is **Elena** with **Tomlinson & Co**.
I wanted to make one final courteous follow-up on your homeowners quote request.
If you still want options, we’re ready to help. If not, I can close this out for now — whichever you prefer."

**If interested:**
"Perfect, happy to help. Let’s finish your details quickly and move this forward."

**If not interested:**
"Understood, and thank you for letting me know.
We’ll pause outreach now. If you ever want a policy review, we’re here."

### Final Close-Out Voicemail
"Hi, this is **Elena** with **Tomlinson & Co**.
This is a final follow-up on your homeowners quote request.
If you’d like to continue, call or text us at [NUMBER].
If now isn’t the right time, no problem — we can close this out for now.
Again, Elena at Tomlinson & Co, [NUMBER]."

## Compliance/Control Lines (insert as needed)

### Consent confirmation
"Just to confirm, is this the best number for quote-related updates?"

### Opt-out language (SMS/call follow-up)
"If you’d rather not receive follow-ups, just tell me and I’ll update that immediately."

### Quiet-hours fallback
"I can follow up tomorrow during normal business hours if that’s better for you."

---

## Recommended cadence
- **Day 0:** Avery first contact
- **Day 1–3:** Mason follow-up
- **Day 7–14:** Elena gentle close-out
- Stop immediately on opt-out/not interested
