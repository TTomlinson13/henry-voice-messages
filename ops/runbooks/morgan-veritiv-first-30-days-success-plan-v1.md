First 30 Days Success Plan (Veritiv)

Days 1-7: Learn the map fast
- Understand your exact role KPIs (revenue, margin, retention, new logos, category mix).
- Build your account universe into A/B/C tiers by revenue potential and urgency.
- Identify top 10 active accounts and top 10 target accounts.
- Meet internal partners: operations, customer service, pricing, and key product specialists.
- Learn your top 20 SKUs and common substitutes by category.

Output by Day 7:
- One-page territory/account map
- Top 10 growth list with next action for each

Days 8-14: Build pipeline and message discipline
- Run structured discovery calls on top opportunities.
- Use one consistent value framework: total cost, service reliability, and process efficiency.
- Track competitor presence account-by-account (Uline/Bunzl/Imperial/etc.).
- Set follow-up cadence (same day recap, 48-hour follow-up, 7-day checkpoint).
- Partner with internal team for quick win proposals.

Output by Day 14:
- Active opportunity list with stage + next step date
- 3 quick-win proposals in flight

Days 15-21: Convert and prove value
- Focus on "pilot-first" wins (single category or location first).
- Quantify value in writing: fill rate, stockout reduction, packaging/labor impact.
- Push decision clarity: yes/no/date, not indefinite maybes.
- Standardize objections handling for price-only conversations.

Output by Day 21:
- 1-2 pilot conversions or approved tests
- KPI baseline sheet for each pilot

Days 22-30: Establish repeatable operating rhythm
- Create weekly account review cadence (Top 15 accounts every week).
- Build a monthly scorecard: pipeline, close rate, growth accounts, risks.
- Identify 3 accounts at churn risk and execute save plans.
- Lock your 60-day plan based on what converted in month one.

Output by Day 30:
- Monthly scorecard
- 60-day focused growth plan

Daily operating checklist (15 minutes)
- 3 must-win actions for the day
- Follow-ups due today
- New outreach count target
- One internal unblock request

Weekly KPI starter set
- New opportunities created
- Pipeline value by stage
- Conversion rate by stage
- Avg days in stage
- Net new revenue (or qualified growth indicators)
- At-risk accounts flagged and actioned

Talk track (quick)
- "I can help you reduce total delivered cost and service volatility, not just unit price."
- "Let’s benchmark one category first and prove results before expanding."
