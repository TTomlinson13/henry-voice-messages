# Florida Homeowner Lead Plan

## Goal
Build a repeatable homeowner lead engine using:
1) Partner network (loan officers, realtors, closing agents)
2) Digital inbound (ads + Canopy link)
3) AI-assisted qualification + follow-up

---

## 1) Lead Engine Structure

### Channel A: Partner Referrals (highest intent)
- Source: loan officer/realtor/closing agent database
- Offer: faster quote turnaround + smoother closings + policy review support
- Conversion path: partner-specific Canopy link

### Channel B: Homeowner Direct Response
- Meta/Google local ads targeting FL homeowners
- Message: rate pressure + renewal review + local expert
- Conversion path: public Canopy link

### Channel C: Reactivation / Renewal Push
- Existing house-file + referral audiences
- Message: “shop before renewal”
- Conversion path: Canopy intake + priority callback SLA

---

## 2) 30-Day Rollout

### Week 1: Foundation
1. Segment partner list into A/B/C tiers
2. Create 3 outreach scripts (LO / realtor / closing agent)
3. Set unique tracking links (UTM tags) per partner/channel
4. Define SLA:
   - New lead touched in 10–15 min
   - Escalate at 30 min

### Week 2: Launch Partner Sprint
5. Contact first 50 A-tier partners
6. Book 10–15 intro calls
7. Offer co-branded intake link + “fast quote support” promise
8. Start weekly partner follow-up cadence

### Week 3: Launch Digital Ads
9. Launch 3 ad sets (Renewal, Overpaying, New Homeowner)
10. Drive all traffic to Canopy link
11. Add retargeting for non-converters
12. Begin daily ad + lead quality review

### Week 4: Optimize
13. Double down on top converting partner types
14. Pause weak ad creatives, scale best 2–3
15. Tighten scripts based on objection patterns
16. Publish weekly scorecard + next actions

---

## 3) AI Agent Roles (Operational)

### Agent 1: Partner Research/Scoring
- Ranks partners by fit + likely volume
- Outputs priority outreach queue daily

### Agent 2: Outreach Composer
- Generates personalized messages by persona
- Maintains script variants for A/B testing

### Agent 3: Follow-up Orchestrator
- Tracks no-response, replied, meeting-booked
- Triggers next-touch reminders

### Agent 4: Lead Ops Analyst
- Reviews lead speed, quality, and close signals
- Flags bottlenecks and recommends fixes weekly

---

## 4) Script Starters (Short)

### Loan Officer
“Hey [Name], we help Florida buyers close faster by handling homeowners quotes quickly and cleanly. Want a simple referral workflow with same-day response?”

### Realtor
“Hey [Name], we help your buyers avoid last-minute insurance issues by giving quick, reliable homeowner options. Want a streamlined referral process?”

### Closing Agent
“Hey [Name], we support smoother closings with fast insurance turnaround and clear communication. Open to a quick intro on how we can support your files?”

---

## 5) KPI Scorecard (Weekly)

- New partner outreaches
- Partner replies
- Intro calls booked
- Active referring partners
- Partner-sourced leads
- Lead-to-quote %
- Quote-to-bind %
- Median first-response time
- Cost per lead (digital)
- Revenue per lead

---

## 6) Compliance Guardrails (Critical)

- Respect DNC/TCPA/calling windows
- Use consent-first outreach where required
- Maintain source + timestamp audit trail
- Suppression lists always on
- No aggressive scraping against site terms
