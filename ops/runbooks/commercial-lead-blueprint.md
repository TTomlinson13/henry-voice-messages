# Commercial Lead Blueprint (30-Day Pilot)

## Objective
Build a repeatable commercial lead-generation engine that prioritizes quality over volume, pre-qualifies leads with AI, and routes producer time to high-probability opportunities.

---

## 1) How Commercial Differs (Operating Assumptions)
- Longer sales cycle
- More underwriting/intake data required
- Higher premium per win, lower lead volume
- More stakeholders in the buying process
- Trust + niche expertise matter more than speed alone

---

## 2) Niche-First Strategy (Start with 3 Verticals)
Pick 3 verticals to pilot first (example):
1. Contractors
2. Habitational / Property Owners
3. Restaurants / Hospitality

### Vertical Selection Criteria
- Existing carrier appetite strength
- Team expertise/confidence
- Average premium size
- Win-rate potential
- Referral density in your network

---

## 3) Commercial Offer Stack (Lead Magnets)
Use consultative offers, not generic “get a quote” only.

1. Free Coverage Gap Review
2. 15-Minute Risk Snapshot
3. Renewal Readiness Check (60 days before renewal)
4. COI/contract compliance quick check (for contractors)
5. Mod + payroll sanity check (where applicable)

---

## 4) Channel Mix

### A) Partner Channels (Primary)
- Commercial lenders / loan officers
- CPAs / bookkeepers
- Business attorneys
- Trade associations
- Commercial real estate / property managers

### B) Outbound Data Channels
- Expired commercial leads
- Local business databases by SIC/NAICS
- Secretary of State new business filings (where practical)

### C) Inbound Digital
- Niche landing pages by vertical
- Search + retargeting
- Educational content (checklists by vertical)

---

## 5) AI Agent Model (Commercial)

### 1. Commercial Intake Agent
- Collects required underwriting fields
- Classifies lead by vertical and complexity
- Flags missing docs

### 2. Follow-Up Agent (Non-annoying cadence)
- Nudges for missing docs/data
- Stops on no-fit/opt-out
- Escalates engaged leads to producer

### 3. Compliance Guardrail Agent
- Channel permissions + quiet hours + consent
- Audit trail for every touchpoint

### 4. Producer Prep Agent
- Builds one-page account brief before producer call
- Includes risk highlights + likely carrier path

### 5. Pipeline Coach Agent
- Daily stuck-deal report
- Next best action recommendations

---

## 6) Commercial “Quote-Ready” Minimum Data
- Legal business name + DBA
- Business address(es)
- Contact name + role
- Email + mobile
- Years in business
- NAICS/SIC or operations description
- Estimated annual revenue
- Estimated payroll
- Current carrier + premium (if known)
- Loss history status (available/pending)
- Effective date / renewal date

Optional by class:
- Fleet details
- Subcontractor usage
- Property characteristics
- Employee count by class

---

## 7) 30-Day Pilot Execution Plan

### Week 1: Foundation
- Select 3 niches
- Build intake form per niche
- Create partner list (top 50)
- Define SLA and status stages

### Week 2: Launch Partner Outreach
- Outreach to 50 partners (segmented)
- Book 10+ intros
- Deploy partner-specific intake links
- Start weekly partner cadence

### Week 3: Activate Digital + Reactivation
- Launch niche ads/landing pages
- Start expired lead reactivation sequence
- Monitor data completeness + response rates

### Week 4: Optimize & Scale
- Double down on best niche/channel
- Remove low-quality sources
- Improve scripts and objections handling
- Publish weekly scorecard and expansion plan

---

## 8) Commercial Follow-Up Cadence (Default)
- Day 0: Intro + value offer + intake link
- Day 2: Missing-data follow-up
- Day 5: Case-example/value message
- Day 10: Renewal/risk prompt
- Day 14: Final soft close-out

Rules:
- Stop on opt-out
- Pause on engagement
- Route hot leads to producer immediately

---

## 9) KPI Scorecard (Weekly)
- Leads by niche and source
- Contact rate
- Data-complete rate
- Quote-ready rate
- Lead-to-producer-call time
- Proposal rate
- Bind rate
- Avg premium won
- Revenue per source
- Unsubscribe/opt-out rate

---

## 10) 10 Immediate Actions
1. Pick top 3 commercial niches
2. Build intake checklists per niche
3. Create partner outreach scripts by role
4. Generate partner tracking links (UTM)
5. Activate compliance suppression checks
6. Launch first 20 partner outreaches
7. Import expired commercial segment for reactivation
8. Set daily 15-min commercial pipeline standup
9. Route quote-ready leads to producer queue
10. Publish first weekly scorecard

---

## Suggested Pilot Constraints
- Start with one geography cluster and 3 niches
- Keep outbound volume controlled until compliance and conversion metrics are stable
- Prioritize quality conversations over raw contact count
