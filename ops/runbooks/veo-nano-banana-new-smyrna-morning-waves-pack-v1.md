Video Lane (Veo)

Cinematic sunrise at New Smyrna Beach, Florida. - Aspect ratio: 16:9

- Style: realistic, cinematic, minimal motion blur
- Negative prompt: no text overlays, no logos, no heavy color cast, no jitter, no artifacts

Version B - 9:16 (Reels/Shorts)
- Duration: 8-10 seconds

- Frame focus: wave edge crossing lower third, sunrise glow upper third

Version C - 1:1 (Feed)

- Aspect ratio: 1:1
