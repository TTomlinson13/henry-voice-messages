# ACORD 125 Kickoff Packet (v1)

## Objective (This Week)
Launch ACORD 125 prototype for Commercial Intake-to-ACORD Compiler with a controlled 10-submission pilot.

## Required Field Dictionary (Quote-Ready Minimum)
1. Legal insured name
2. DBA (if any)
3. FEIN
4. Primary contact name/title
5. Email + phone
6. Mailing address
7. Operations description
8. NAICS/classification
9. Years in business
10. Annual revenue
11. Payroll total
12. Employee count
13. Requested effective date
14. Prior carrier/premium (or unknown attestation)
15. 5-year loss summary (or no-loss attestation)

## Quote-Ready Gate
Submission is Quote-Ready only if all required fields are complete and no critical conflicts are present.

Critical conflicts:
- revenue or payroll missing/invalid
- effective date invalid
- contradictory exposure values

## Pilot Cohort (First 10)
Selection criteria:
- 6 standard commercial submissions
- 2 incomplete/messy submissions
- 2 loss-history-heavy submissions

For each pilot file, track:
- intake completeness score
- auto-map confidence score
- exception count
- reviewer correction time

## Review Workflow

3. Exception queue (if needed)
4. Maria review/approval
