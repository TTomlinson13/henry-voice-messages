# Escalation Matrix (Starter)

## Severity Definitions
- **S1 (Critical):** immediate financial/coverage harm risk or legal/regulatory threat.
- **S2 (High):** deadline within 24h, cancellation risk, unresolved client-impacting issue.
- **S3 (Normal):** routine service/sales processing.

## Routing

### S1
- Immediate owner: Todd or delegated senior lead
- SLA: acknowledge <= 15 minutes
- Automation: no auto-send beyond acknowledgment; route to L3

### S2
- Owner: Henry + assigned ops manager
- SLA: acknowledge <= 60 minutes
- Automation: L1 drafts allowed; L2 only for pre-approved templates

### S3
- Owner: automation queue + assigned specialist
- SLA: same business day
- Automation: L1/L2 according to intent policy

## Escalation Triggers
- complaint/legal language
- cancellation date same day
- repeat contact without resolution > 2 business interactions
- high-value client tag + unresolved issue > 4 hours

## Communication Pattern
1. Acknowledge receipt
2. State next action + ETA
3. Confirm owner
4. Follow through with closure note
