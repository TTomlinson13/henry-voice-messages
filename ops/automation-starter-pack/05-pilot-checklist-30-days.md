# 30-Day Pilot Checklist

## Week 1 — Foundation
- [ ] Confirm intent taxonomy
- [ ] Approve autonomy ladder policy
- [ ] Tag VIP clients/senders
- [ ] Finalize 10 email templates + 5 phone flows
- [ ] Define prohibited keywords list

## Week 2 — L1 Draft Pilot (No Auto-Send)
- [ ] Turn on triage agent (read/classify)
- [ ] Turn on drafting agent for top 5 intents
- [ ] Route all drafts to approval queue
- [ ] Measure: draft usefulness, edit %, response-time impact

## Week 3 — Narrow L2 Pilot
- [ ] Pick 1-2 low-risk intents for conditional auto-send
- [ ] Enable confidence + required-field gates
- [ ] Audit every outbound automatically
- [ ] Daily 10-minute review of L2 outcomes

## Week 4 — Evaluate + Scale/Correct
- [ ] Weekly KPI review
- [ ] Keep/widen/restrict L2 by intent
- [ ] Add one additional approved intent if quality >= target
- [ ] Document misses and rule updates

## KPI Targets (Starter)
- Draft acceptance rate >= 70%
- Human edit distance trending down week-over-week
- Zero compliance incidents
- P0/P1 acknowledgment SLA hit >= 95%

## Go/No-Go for Expansion
Expand only if:
- SLA and quality targets met
- No high-severity misses
- Team confidence positive

Otherwise: revert specific intents to L1 and tighten rules.
